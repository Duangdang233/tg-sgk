# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Please be respectful, constructive, and collaborative in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent
- Give actionable technical feedback
- Respect privacy and security boundaries
- Keep discussion focused on the project

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Sharing private credentials, session data, or personal Telegram data
- Encouraging misuse outside the project's documented safety boundaries

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for behavior that harms the community or violates the project's security expectations.
